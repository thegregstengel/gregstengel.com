The 2.3.0 release notes open with a thank-you: most of the release "came straight from the Discord". That set the pattern for the next sixteen days. Between September 22 and October 8, 2026, eight feature releases went out, and players shaped nearly all of them.

## What players asked for

**Planets that collect themselves (2.3).** Production now goes straight into a planet's storage. Nobody has to fly around every cycle to press Collect.

**More mines (2.3).** The cap went from 50 to 500 per captain.

**A way to talk (2.5).** The Feed tab became Comms, with channels for the galaxy, your corp, and direct hails. Posting to the galaxy became free, with `@` mentions.

**A way out of a bounty (2.6).** Captains on the wrong side of a faction could not reach its registry to pay off a warrant, so the bounty never went away. Now you can always come in to settle it.

**Turns that wait for you (2.7).** You still get 250 turns every four hours, but unused turns now bank, up to 1,000. Log in once a day and they are all there.

## What needed rebalancing

Smuggling paid too well. Release 2.3 tied black-market prices to the local market, and 2.7 cut the load sizes. Smuggling still pays more than honest trading, just not four times more.

## Dominion

The last release of the run, 2.10, added a new way to win.

XP shows how hard you've played. Credits show how rich you are. Dominion measures how much of the galaxy you hold: ports taken, planets built up, starbases raised. It scores every four hours, so an empire held for weeks beats a quick grab.

It shipped as a preview. Next season it counts toward the podium.
