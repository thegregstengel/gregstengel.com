Planets, Fleets, and Mines: Six Days of Big Bang Smugglers 1.x

Three Big Bang Smugglers releases in six days added planet sieges, ship destruction, corporation fleets, and deployable mines.

Posted Jun 18, 2026
By Stengel , Claude
1 min read

Planets, Fleets, and Mines: Six Days of Big Bang Smugglers 1.x

This post was written after the fact, from the release notes.

Between June 13 and June 18, 2026, Big Bang Smugglers shipped three feature releases. Together they turned a trading game into one where you can lose things.

1.18: Planets 2.0

Planets stopped being passive income and became something to fight over.

  • Siege combat and planet capture. Someone else’s planet can become yours.
  • Barracks and a Citadel. Barracks produce fighters. The Citadel is the capstone.
  • Port attacks. Ports can be destroyed, and they respawn.
  • Cloaking devices, plus upgrade tiers for fighters, torpedoes, and shields.
  • Cinematics. Cut scenes for milestones and travel.

1.19: Fleets, Garages & Destruction

This is the release where ships started blowing up.

  • Ship destruction and escape pods. Lose the fight, ride the pod home.
  • More than one ship. Garages and Starbase storage let you own, swap, buy, and sell ships.
  • Corporation fleets. Corps can form up and fight together, with shared loot.
  • Private corp chat, and an instant-travel Tesseract Drive for sectors you’ve visited.

1.20: Limpet Tracker and Deployable Mines

The last of the three made the map dangerous.

  • Limpet trackers. Buy one at a port, plant it in a sector, and it latches onto ships that warp through. It reports where they are until they sweep it off.
  • Mines that trigger on warp. Before this, warping into a mined sector skipped the mines entirely.
  • Notifications when one of your mines goes off.

Why it mattered

Until this point, the worst thing that could happen to you was a bad trade. After it, other captains could take your planet, destroy your ship, and follow you around the galaxy.

These were also some of the last big features built on the original platform. Six weeks later the whole game was rebuilt.
