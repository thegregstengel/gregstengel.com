Version 2.0 rebuilt the engine. Version 3.0, released on October 21, 2026, rebuilt everything you look at.

## STARNAV 2002 is a chart now

The scanner used to be a list. Now it is a map of everywhere you have been.

- **It remembers.** Every sector you've visited, scanned, or bought intel on stays on your chart, with how long ago you saw it.
- **You fly from it.** Tap a sector and one card shows what you know and what you can do: move, warp, plan a route, scan, drop a beacon. Every button shows its turn cost.
- **It shows who holds what.** Grey is unexplored. Green is explored. Blue is yours or your corp's. Red is where an enemy was when you last looked.
- **It remembers prices**, and how old they are.

Sectors visited before 3.0 were filled in on day one.

## Your own bridge

Players can hide, move, and fold the panels on every screen. The layout saves to the account, so the web game and the Android app match, and it carries across seasons.

## One game, one look

Every card, sheet, and pop-up was rebuilt on a single design. The coloured edges mean something:

- **Gold** needs you.
- **Red** is danger.
- **Blue** is the Federation.
- **Green** is money.

Tabs were merged too. Port and Stations became Dock. Your corporation's chat, members, and assets now live in one place under Comms.

## Five days later

Version 3.1 followed on October 26, built from the Discord suggestions channel. You can now buy several upgrade levels at once, and warp to any sector where your corporation owns a planet.
