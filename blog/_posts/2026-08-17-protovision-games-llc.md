On August 17, 2026, Protovision Games became Protovision Games LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

Until that day it was a name on a website. Now it is a company.

The filing came sixteen days after [Big Bang Smugglers 2.0]({% post_url 2026-08-01-big-bang-smugglers-2-0-a-new-universe %}) launched.

For players, nothing changes. The games are the same and so is the person making them.

It is still one person making games after hours. It just has paperwork now.
